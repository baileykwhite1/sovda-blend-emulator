Training

Load & Blend, wait for Blending Complete, then Unload. Watch which door opens and how long the drum takes to empty.

The roastery feeds the intake by hand. Turn both options off in Blender Settings and check the three timing drums grey out.

Change the default unload door to door 3 and run a cycle to prove it. Door 3 must stay enabled or the machine cannot unload.

Blending Speed down to 30 Hz in Configuration Settings, Blend Time up to 0:45 on the Settings page. Use the keypad for at least one of them.

Times 0:29 / 0:32 / 0:10, all doors enabled, door 1 default, sensor and lift on, 40 Hz / 20 Hz.

Keyboard & notes
